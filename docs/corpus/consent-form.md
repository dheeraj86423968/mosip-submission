## Part A — Information sheet (please read before signing)

**What is this about?** We are testing software that checks whether the
face in front of a camera belongs to a live person. The alternative it
looks for is a photo, a screen, a video or a mask. To test it, we need
short videos of real faces, and we need to try to fool the software with
copies of those faces.

**What will happen?** The session takes about 15 minutes.

1. We record a few short videos (about 10 seconds each) of your face, on
   a laptop camera and a phone camera, under different lighting. **No
   sound is recorded.**
2. From one of those videos we make "attack instruments": paper prints of
   your face, a print with the eye area cut out, a photo and a video of
   you shown on a phone or tablet screen, and possibly a paper mask. A
   team member may hold or wear these in front of the camera.
3. **All prints and masks are shredded before you leave, in front of you.**
   Copies on phones and tablets are deleted in front of you.
4. If you agree (box 5), you choose your own skin-tone band from a printed
   scale, and your age band and a few appearance details such as glasses
   or head covering. We use these only to check that the software works
   equally well for different people.

**Who can take part?** Adults aged **18 or over** who can read this sheet,
or have had it read to them, in a language they understand. People under
18 cannot take part.

**Is it voluntary?** Yes. You may refuse, skip any optional box, or stop
at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence. Your
relationship with the team, your course or your college is not affected.

**What happens to my data?**
* Your videos are labelled with a code such as `P07`, never with your
  name. Only this paper form links your name to the code. It is kept
  locked by the data controller and is never scanned to a phone or the
  cloud.
* The videos are stored only on an **encrypted drive** held by two named
  team members. They are **not** uploaded to any cloud service, never put
  in the project's public code repository, and never shared outside the
  team.
* What we publish are **only overall numbers** (for example "the software
  rejected 97 % of printed photos"). No image of you and no individual
  result is ever published.
* The videos are **permanently deleted on or before 31 January 2027**.
  This form is kept for 3 years as proof that you agreed, then shredded.

**Risks.** The main risk is that your face images could leak. We reduce it
with encryption, a short retention period, no cloud storage, and
destroying the attack instruments on the day. If a leak happens anyway,
we will tell you within 72 hours.

**Benefits.** There is no direct benefit to you. The work helps make
national ID registration harder to cheat, and checks that it treats people
fairly.

**Withdrawing later.** Contact the withdrawal contact above and quote your
participant code. We delete all your videos and scores within **7 days**
and confirm to you. Overall numbers that were already published cannot be
recalled, but they contain nothing that identifies you, and later results
will not include you.
